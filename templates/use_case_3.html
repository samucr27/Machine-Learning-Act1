Use Case 3: Personalized Product Recommendations

Problem Context

Online stores offer thousands of products, making it hard for customers to find items that match their interests. Without guidance, customers may leave the platform without purchasing, resulting in lost sales opportunities.

Data That Could Be Used

Customer browsing history, past purchases, product ratings, time spent on product pages, and similar customers' purchasing patterns.

What the Model Should Predict, Classify, or Discover

The model discovers hidden relationships between products and customer preferences to generate personalized product suggestions for each user.

Type of Machine Learning Applied

Unsupervised Learning — the model groups customers and products based on similarity patterns without needing predefined labels.

Potential Benefit

Stores increase sales and customer satisfaction by showing relevant products, while customers save time finding what they actually want.
