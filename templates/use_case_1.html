Use Case 1: Early Disease Risk Detection

Problem Context

Hospitals need to identify patients at high risk of developing chronic conditions like diabetes or heart disease before symptoms become severe. Traditional risk assessment relies on manual review of patient history, which is slow and often misses subtle patterns across multiple health indicators.

Data That Could Be Used

Patient records including age, blood pressure, cholesterol levels, glucose readings, BMI, family medical history, and lifestyle factors such as smoking or physical activity.

What the Model Should Predict, Classify, or Discover

The model predicts the probability that a patient will develop a specific condition within a defined time period, classifying patients into risk categories (low, medium, high).

Type of Machine Learning Applied

Supervised Learning — historical patient data with known diagnoses can be used as labeled examples to train the model.

Potential Benefit

Doctors can intervene earlier with preventive care, reducing treatment costs and improving patient outcomes through timely diagnosis.
