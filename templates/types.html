Types of Machine Learning

There are different types of Machine Learning. Each type learns from data in a different way. Below are the four main types.

1. Supervised Learning

In Supervised Learning, the computer learns from data that has labels. A label is the correct answer. The computer sees many examples with answers, and it learns to give answers for new data.

Characteristics: It needs labeled data. It learns to predict a specific answer.

Example: An email app learns to detect spam. It looks at many emails marked as "spam" or "not spam" and learns the pattern.

2. Unsupervised Learning

In Unsupervised Learning, the computer learns from data without labels. There are no correct answers. The computer finds patterns and groups by itself.

Characteristics: It does not need labeled data. It finds hidden patterns or groups in the data.

Example: A store studies customers and groups them by their shopping habits, without knowing the groups in advance.
