Use Case 4: Autonomous Vehicle Navigation

Problem Context

Self-driving cars must make split-second decisions in constantly changing traffic conditions, including avoiding obstacles, following traffic rules, and adapting to unpredictable driver or pedestrian behavior. Programming fixed responses for every possible scenario is not feasible.

Data That Could Be Used

Real-time sensor data from cameras, LIDAR, GPS, speed, and surrounding vehicle positions, along with feedback from previous driving decisions.

What the Model Should Predict, Classify, or Discover

The model learns the optimal action to take at each moment, such as accelerating, braking, or turning, to safely reach a destination.

Type of Machine Learning Applied

Reinforcement Learning — the vehicle learns through trial and error by receiving rewards for safe driving and penalties for mistakes.

Potential Benefit

Reduces traffic accidents caused by human error and improves transportation efficiency, offering safer mobility for passengers and pedestrians.
